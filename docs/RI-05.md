# RI-05: the 5 % as code, scored on RI-04's own tape

Written 2026-10-27. RI-05 is the 5 % (`docs/INSENTOS-DUVAL.md` Part 2, applied under the three Dwarkesh interviews in
`docs/FIVE-PERCENT-RSI.md`) turned into rules a tape can score. It is a shadow book beside RI-04, paper only, never counted in
RI-04's leg. Ruled 2026-10-27 (`decisions/2026-10-27-ri05-five-percent-shadow.md`): **RI-05 stays a replayed hypothesis book.**
Nothing is wired into the counted engine until a replay shows the loop at PF 1 or better.

- `src/five.py`: every rule as a function, plus a `Shadow` class that could run live on the engine's marks (complete, uncalled).
- `ops/five_replay.py`: walks `journal/paths/<day>.jsonl` and `journal/news/<day>.jsonl` in time order, runs RI-05 and RI-04's
  control over the same launches with the same curve arithmetic and fees, and splits the control by the loop's own reads.
  Writes `out/five.md` and `state/five_replay.json`. Runs daily at 05:50 (task `rich - FIVE`) and inside the `/rich` morning command.

```
python ops/five_replay.py --days 5
```

## 1. The rules as code

| item | the 5 % says | the code reads | constant |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | price is the flow of attention; trade the second derivative | flow = change in the curve's real SOL over 10 s; accel = change in flow; S-curve phase from (flow, accel): slow, exp, linear, log, flat | `FLOW_W` 10 s, `ACCEL_EPS` 0.02 SOL/s |
| L1 | is there a live meta today? if not, do not trade | runner share of the trailing hour (plain pump launches that touched +20 %); under 0.30 the hour is dead; under 30 launches the gate is unknown and unknown is closed | `GATE_DEAD` 0.30, `GATE_MIN_N` 30, `RUNNER_MULT` 1.2 |
| L2 | classify before the chart; only narrative is tradable | dev: creator bought 2 SOL or more at create; tech: a TECH word in the name; celebrity: claims official, verified or real; narrative: a news-matched name that is none of those; none: unmatched | `DEV_SOL` 2.0, `TECH`, `OFFICIAL` |
| L3 | name the comparable and the next catalyst; blanks mean a scalp or zero | comparable: a prior launch of the last 24 h carrying the same term, its ceiling the best peak multiple; catalyst clock: the term's age in the hour's news; blank comparable: scalp; comparable that never ran: zero | `COMP_W` 86400 s, `CATALYST_FRESH_S` 1800 s, `TERM_GAP_S` 600 s |
| L4 | enter with the free-trade structure; initials out early | at +20 % sell exactly enough tokens to take the stake back; the remainder rides at zero cost | `LEG1` 0.20 |
| L5 | three triggers decided at entry | (a) catalyst lands: the term leaves the hour's news; (b) arrivals slow: flow at or under zero for 10 s; (c) resistance: the 15-minute runner share under 0.20 closes everything and blocks entries for 15 minutes; plus the physics floor -5 % before the stake is out, and the 180 s window after | `SLOW_S` 10, `GATE_RESIST` 0.20, `RESIST_W` 900, `STOP` -0.05, `HOLD_S` 180 |
| N1 | never hold through the catalyst | trigger (a) closes the whole remainder | |
| N2 | never trade dev, tech, celebrity | those classes are skipped at entry | |
| N3 | vamps only on a held narrative | a second launch on a term already held is a scalp, otherwise skipped | `SCALP_SOL` 0.5 |
| N4 | never add money after a round trip | a 6 SOL realised drawdown halves the stake for the rest of the contract | `RESET_DD` 6.0, `SIZE_SOL` 1.5 |

Scalps use RI-04's own exits (+20 %, -5 %, 60 s). Fills are the curve's own (`duval.buy`, `duval.sell`, `duval.net_in`) with
the 1 % pump fee and the tx fee each side, the same as the counted book. Paths are 180 s long, so no replayed hold can exceed
180 s; L4's "ride to the catalyst" past three minutes cannot be scored by replay.

## 3. What was ruled, and what is owed

Ruled 2026-10-27 (`decisions/2026-10-27-ri05-five-percent-shadow.md`, option A of three: replay only; wire the shadow live; drop it):

- RI-05 stays a replayed hypothesis book. `Shadow` is not called from `src/duval.py`.
- The creator's buy at create (I03 in the decision) is a forecast for the Foresight Journal on RI-04's own closes before it is a
  rule for any contract. It needs a resolver that reads `init_sol` from the paths file; not written yet.

Ruled 2026-10-27 (`decisions/2026-10-27-ri05-stop-stage.md`, option A of three: align the entry and show both fills; loosen the
stop; first mark for both books):

- RI-05 fills at the create message's reserves (`five.entry_marks`, and the same state in `Shadow`), which the ruling believed was
  where the counted book fills; it is not (section 5). The stop stays at RI-04's -5 %.
- The report shows both books at both fills every morning. The gate `rich-ri05-positive-on-tape` now reads PF 4.22, in-sample on
  the days the constants were frozen; a second gate, `rich-ri05-out-of-sample-days`, holds wiring the shadow until the aligned
  replay has scored at least one day from 2026-10-28 on.
- The fill question goes to the Foresight Journal as the first-buyer forecast RI-04 owes; RI-04's running contract is not changed
  by anything found here.
- Open: whether the dev-class edge survives to 24 h (the ret24 file could say), and whether the "narrative" insentos and Duval
  trade is the Google News proper noun RI-04 matches at all (U02, carried from 2026-10-23).

Nothing here changes a counted trade. RI-04's rules change only through RI.

## 5. 2026-10-28: the grader was a phantom

Ruled `decisions/2026-10-28-ri04-fill-grader.md` (option A of three: grade at the attainable fill; keep the message-fill headline;
stop the counted engine). Found while running the daily operating checklist (rule 1, name the one binding stage; rule 2, reality
is the only grader) on the day's results.

**What the engine does.** `duval.Book.enter` fills at the curve's reserves as read on chain at decision (Amendment 1 of 10-23),
not at the create message's reserves. The population control, RI-05 (`five.entry_marks`) and the 10-27 ruling fill at the message
state and said the counted book did too. The marks were never the problem: 10-27's 196 counted closes replayed along the paths
with the live arithmetic land within 0.02 SOL on 195 of them.

**What the join says** (`journal/paper.jsonl` to `journal/paths`, 1150 of 1153 closes, 10-23..10-28):

| counted entries | n | win | sum SOL | PF |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| got the message state (no buyer before the chain read; median 0.0 s after create) | 588 (51 %) | 13 % | -7.3 | 0.71 |
| an earlier buyer had moved the curve | 562 (49 %) | 18 % | -45.6 | 0.50 |
| the same 562 refilled at the message state (what the control books) | 562 | 63 % | +208.8 | 9.30 |

The control's edge (+97 SOL on 650 in the morning report, +201 SOL over all 1150) is the earlier buyer's pump, booked as if the
book had bought before them. On the half of entries where the book really was first, the loop as written loses. So the
first-buyer question is answered as far as paper can answer it: being first loses less, not more than nothing.

**What changed.** The replay headlines both books at the first on-chain mark (the state nearest the counted book's fill) and shows
the message fill as a labelled counterfactual. At that fill RI-04's control is PF 0.45 and RI-05 PF 0.31 over the whole tape, in
agreement with the counted book, which has been KILL since 16:56Z 10-23 (G1 at the 11th close, G2 at 20:11Z) and stands at
-52.5 SOL on 1153 closes. Belief B9 moved to 0.10. Two lessons (measurement: the population printed this gap on 10-24 with the
contract's own line that it is a lesson owed, and none was written; model: the loop loses when first) and three forecasts (the
counted mean on 10-31; the first-fill share and its mean over 10-28..10-30) are in the Foresight Journal. RI-04's contract and its
verdict are untouched. Stopping the engine is the operator's call: `src/duval.py` also writes the paths tape every replay needs.
RI-05 stays a hypothesis scorer with nothing to promote; the `rich-ri05-positive-on-tape` gate, read at the attainable fill, is
PF 0.31 and fails.
